Notes
-----
- Figures are PNG at ~300 dpi at their print widths (the multi-panel figures are
  2000-2400 px wide). If the system requires TIFF/EPS or a higher nominal dpi,
  the plotting scripts can re-export; Figures 4-5 use random subsampling, so a
  re-run will shift those curves slightly (the reported numbers stay the same).
- The Tables .docx files are editable Word tables, each with its caption.
