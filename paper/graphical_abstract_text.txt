Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

On four kinase profiling datasets, the rankings given by the S-score, entropy,
Gini coefficient and fold-selectivity ratio depend on choices usually left
implicit, such as where the activity boundary is placed. No measure examined is
robust to that boundary, so it must be fixed by protocol. A gated, smooth-hinge
entropy is monotone when a weakly bound kinase is added while still weighting
weak binding, and ranks compounds almost as the entropy does.
