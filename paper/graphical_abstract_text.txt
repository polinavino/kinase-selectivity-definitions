Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Required Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

Across four kinase profiling datasets and three assay technologies, selectivity
definitions cluster into two families that measure different properties, and
rank instability is predictable from a compound's binding profile. We (1) identify
three distinct instability sources, (2) propose four properties that any
well-formed selectivity measure should satisfy, and (3) propose a measure that
satisfies all four, which no existing definition does.
