Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

We propose four properties a well-formed kinase inhibitor selectivity measure
should satisfy, motivated by three sources of rank instability on four
profiling datasets; none of the S-score, entropy, Gini coefficient or fold-
selectivity ratio satisfies all of them. With entropy and Gini on a common
log-affinity scale, the first three agree more with one another than with the
ratio. A gated variant of the entropy satisfies all four when its activity
boundary is fixed at the assay floor.
