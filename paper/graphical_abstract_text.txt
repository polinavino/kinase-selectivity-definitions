Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

We propose four properties a well-formed kinase inhibitor selectivity measure
should satisfy, motivated by rank instability on four profiling datasets; none
of the S-score, entropy, Gini coefficient or fold-selectivity ratio satisfies
all of them. With entropy and Gini on a common log-affinity scale, the first
three agree more with one another than with the ratio. A gated, smooth-hinge
entropy satisfies all four (one vacuously) and ranks these datasets almost
identically to the entropy.
