Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

Across four kinase profiling datasets spanning three assay technologies,
selectivity definitions split three to one: the S-score, entropy and Gini
agree closely while the fold-selectivity ratio agrees with none of them, and
rank instability is predictable from binding-profile shape. We identify three
instability sources and motivate four properties that none of the four
definitions assessed satisfies, then propose a measure that does.
