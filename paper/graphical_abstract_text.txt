Analysis Choices and Ranking Stability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity Scores

Polina Vinogradova*

On four kinase profiling datasets, rankings by the S-score, entropy, Gini and
ratio depend on choices usually left implicit: how affinities are
weighted, which compounds are ranked, and where the activity boundary is
placed. Moving the activity boundary reorders compounds under every score, and
the boundary must be fixed by protocol. The share of targets with detected
binding, and a gated entropy derived from four proposed criteria, recover
rankings from small panels better than the ratio or the published entropy.
