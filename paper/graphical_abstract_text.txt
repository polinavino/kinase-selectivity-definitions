Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

Across four kinase profiling datasets spanning three assay technologies,
selectivity definitions (entropy and Gini on a common log-affinity scale)
split into two groups: the S-score, entropy and Gini agree more with one
another than with the fold-selectivity ratio, and rank instability is
associated with binding-profile shape. We identify three instability sources
and motivate four properties that none of the four definitions assessed
satisfies, then propose a measure that does when its activity boundary is
fixed at the assay floor.
