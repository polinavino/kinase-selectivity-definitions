Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Required Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

Across four kinase profiling datasets spanning three assay technologies,
selectivity definitions cluster into two families measuring different
properties, and rank instability is predictable from binding-profile shape.
We identify three instability sources and derive four properties that no
existing selectivity measure satisfies, then propose one that does.
