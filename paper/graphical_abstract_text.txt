Analysis Choices and Ranking Stability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity Scores

Polina Vinogradova*

On four kinase profiling datasets, the rankings given by the S-score, entropy,
Gini coefficient and ratio depend on choices usually left
implicit: how affinities are weighted, which compounds are ranked, and where
the activity boundary is placed. No boundary parameter we tested is robust, so
the boundary must be fixed by protocol. Scores that follow the share of targets
with detected binding recover rankings from small panels better than the ratio
or the published entropy.
