Analysis Choices and Ranking Stability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity Scores

Polina Vinogradova*

Kinase selectivity rankings depend on affinity weighting, floor-coded pairs
and the compounds eligible for scoring. Across four datasets, rank agreement
on smaller panels can remain high even when compounds with few active targets
become unscorable, so recovery must be reported with compound coverage. A
conditional characterization of gated entropies gives a monotonicity guarantee
under weak-target addition, but does not establish an empirical recovery
advantage over simpler scores.
