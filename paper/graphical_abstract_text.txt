Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure

Polina Vinogradova*

On four kinase profiling datasets, the rankings given by the S-score, entropy,
Gini coefficient and fold-selectivity ratio depend on choices usually left
implicit, such as where the activity boundary is placed. No measure we assess
is robust to that boundary, so it must be fixed by protocol. Measures that
follow the share of targets with detected binding recover rankings from small
panels better than the ratio or the published entropy.
