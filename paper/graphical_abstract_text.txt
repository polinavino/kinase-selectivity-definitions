Analysis Choices and Ranking Stability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity Scores

Polina Vinogradova*

Kinase selectivity rankings depend on affinity weighting, floor-coded pairs
and the compounds eligible for scoring. Across four datasets, agreement among
compounds retained on small panels can conceal the loss of narrowly active
compounds, so recovery must be reported with coverage. A conditional
characterization of gated entropies gives a monotonicity guarantee under
weak-target addition, but does not establish an empirical recovery advantage
over simpler scores.
