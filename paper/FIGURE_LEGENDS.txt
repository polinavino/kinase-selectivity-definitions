Figure legends (place together at the end of the manuscript, after the
references, per Molecular Informatics style; figures upload as separate files).

Figure 1. Entropy (top) and Gini coefficient (bottom) rankings on Davis and
Klaeger under two operationalizations: association-constant weighting, the
published form of the entropy (x-axis), and the baseline-subtracted log-
affinity form used throughout (y-axis, median rank over the baseline sweep).
Grey: floor-coded pairs kept at the floor; blue: floor-coded pairs given no
weight. Spearman r above each panel.

Figure 2. Spearman correlations between the median rankings of the four
selectivity definitions on each dataset (compounds x targets above each panel).
S: S-score; Ent: entropy. On Metz, rankings largely follow how many kinases
each compound was measured against (Methods).

Figure 3. Within-family rank instability against the profile feature most
strongly correlated with it, for each definition family (Klaeger, compounds
with an active kinase, n=200): active-affinity range (S-score), number of
active kinases (entropy), active-affinity skewness (Gini), and top1-top2 gap
(ratio). Spearman r above each panel.

Figure 4. Binding profiles of three Davis compounds, targets sorted by
affinity. Left: nintedanib, most stable (sigma = 2.1), 153 active targets.
Center: PHA-665752, most unstable (sigma = 16.4), 60 active targets with one
dominant target (Type 3). Right: fedratinib, most ratio-sensitive (Type 2):
JAK2 and GAK tied, among 123 active targets. Dashed line: pKd = 6 activity
threshold; dotted line: pKd = 5 assay floor.

Figure 5. Rank instability on Klaeger. Left (D1): rank standard deviation
across the existing definitions' parameter sweeps, for compounds with no
binding above the activity threshold and for compounds with at least one active
kinase. Right (D2): per-compound rank standard deviation against the number of
active kinases, with one parameter swept over B = [5.0, 6.0]: the entropy's
baseline beta (orange), the candidate's gate x0 with its emphasis baseline
pinned to x0 (green), and the candidate's emphasis baseline with x0 fixed at
the assay floor (red).

Figure 6. Recovery from random subpanels with the D1 gate applied to every
measure, on all four datasets (50 subpanels per size). Top: mean Spearman
correlation between the subpanel and full-panel rankings, over compounds scored
on both. Middle: recoverability, the share of clearly separated full-panel
pairs that are scored on the subpanel and keep their order. Bottom: coverage,
the share of compounds scored on the full panel that are still scored. The
S-score at tau* and the active count coincide. The published entropy is not
defined on Anastassiadis. Dashed line: 0.90.
