Figure legends

Figure 1. Rankings using weights inversely proportional to binding constants (x-axis) against the baseline-subtracted log-affinity form (y-axis, median rank over the baseline sweep), on Davis and Klaeger. Figure 2. Binding profiles of two Davis compounds, targets sorted by affinity. Left: fedratinib. Right: PHA-665752. Dashed line: pK_(d) = 6 activity threshold. Dotted line: pK_(d) = 5 coded floor.

Figure 3. Recovery from random subpanels with D1 applied to every score, on all four datasets (50 subpanels per size). Top: coverage, the share of compounds scored on the full panel that are still scored. Middle: recoverability, the share of clearly separated full-panel pairs that remain scored and keep their order. Their full-panel rank gap is at least a quarter of the number of scored compounds; subpanel ties count as one half. Bottom: mean Spearman correlation between subpanel and full-panel rankings over compounds scored on both, with ±1 standard deviation across subpanels shaded. Each score is compared with its own full-panel ranking. The published entropy and P_(max) give floor-coded pairs zero weight and are not defined on Anastassiadis. Dashed line: 0.90.
