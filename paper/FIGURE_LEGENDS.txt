Figure 1. Rankings under association-constant weights (x-axis) against the
baseline-subtracted log-affinity form (y-axis; median rank over the baseline
sweep), on Davis and Klaeger. Top: entropy, for which association-constant
weights are the published form. Bottom: Gini, shown for comparison; its
published form uses percent inhibition. Grey: floor-coded pairs kept at the
floor; blue: floor-coded pairs given zero weight. Spearman r above each panel.

Figure 2. Binding profiles of two Davis compounds, targets sorted by affinity.
Left: fedratinib, the most ratio-sensitive compound, with JAK2 and GAK tied
among 123 active targets. Right: PHA-665752, the most unstable compound overall
(sigma = 16.4), with one dominant target among 60 active targets. Dashed line:
pKd = 6 activity threshold; dotted line: pKd = 5 assay floor.

Figure 3. Recovery from random subpanels with D1 applied to every score, on all
four datasets (50 subpanels per size). Top: coverage, the share of compounds
scored on the full panel that are still scored. Middle: recoverability, the
share of clearly separated full-panel pairs that are scored on the subpanel and
keep their order. Bottom: mean Spearman correlation between subpanel and
full-panel rankings over compounds scored on both, with +/-1 standard deviation
across subpanels shaded. Each score is compared with its own full-panel
ranking. The published entropy and P_max give floor-coded pairs zero weight
and are not defined on Anastassiadis. Dashed line: 0.90.
