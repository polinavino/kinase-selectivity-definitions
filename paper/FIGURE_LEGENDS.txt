Figure legends (place together at the end of the manuscript, after the

Figure 1. Entropy (top) and Gini coefficient (bottom) rankings on Davis and
Klaeger under two operationalizations: a form close to the original publication
(association-constant weighting, x-axis) and the baseline-subtracted
log-affinity form used throughout (y-axis). Spearman r above each panel.

Figure 2. Spearman correlations between the median rankings of the four
selectivity definitions on each dataset (compounds x kinases above each panel).
S: S-score; Ent: entropy.

Figure 3. Within-family rank instability against the profile feature most
strongly correlated with it, for each definition family (Klaeger, compounds with
an active kinase, n = 206): active-affinity range (S-score), number of active
kinases (entropy), active-affinity skewness (Gini), and top1-top2 gap (ratio).
Spearman r above each panel.

Figure 4. Binding profiles of three Davis compounds, kinases sorted by affinity.
Left: neflamapimod (VX-745), most stable (sigma = 1.8), two active kinases.
Center: PI-103, most unstable (sigma = 19.3), 22 active kinases with no dominant
target. Right: GDC-0879, most ratio-sensitive (Type 2), five active kinases
including two near-tied top targets (delta pKd = 0.023). Dashed line: pKd = 6
activity threshold; dotted line: pKd = 5 assay floor.

Figure 5. Mean Spearman correlation between rankings computed on random kinase
subpanels and rankings on the full panel, as a function of subpanel size, on
all four datasets. Shaded bands indicate +/-1 standard deviation across 50
subsamples; the dashed line marks r = 0.90, and p* (legend) is the smallest
panel reaching mean r > 0.90.

Figure 6. Rank instability on Klaeger. Left (D1): rank standard deviation across
the existing definitions' parameter sweeps, for compounds with no binding above
the activity threshold and for compounds with at least one active kinase. Right
(D3): per-compound rank standard deviation against the number of active kinases,
with one parameter swept over B = [5.0, 6.0]: the entropy's baseline beta
(orange), the candidate's gate x0 with its emphasis baseline pinned to x0
(green), and the candidate's emphasis baseline with x0 fixed at the assay floor
(red).

Figure 7. Panel-size convergence of the candidate measure compared with the four
existing definitions, on all four datasets (50 random subpanels per size).
Curves show the mean Spearman correlation between the ranking computed on a
random subpanel and the full-panel ranking; p* (legend) is the smallest panel
reaching mean r > 0.90.
