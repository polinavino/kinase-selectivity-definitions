Dear Professor Baumann,

I am pleased to submit the second revision of manuscript 7756455, as a
Research Article (Full Paper). Its title is changed from "Definitional
Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical Characterization and
Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure" to "Analysis Choices and
Ranking Stability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity Scores", to describe the
revised content.

I am grateful to the reviewer for a second constructive assessment. In
response, the manuscript has been revised as follows:

  - the manuscript is consolidated around one question, which analysis
    choices change selectivity rankings, and shortened from about 11,100 to
    about 6,600 words, with the full evidence for each check in a
    Supporting Information file that follows the same order;

  - the analyses now use one reference target per human protein kinase,
    removing mutant and domain constructs of the same gene from Davis,
    duplicate complexes from Anastassiadis, and targets that Klaeger et al.
    do not annotate as protein kinases from Klaeger;

  - the published entropy is compared with our log-affinity form under two
    treatments of pairs without detected binding, in both the agreement and
    the small-panel analyses;

  - the criteria and the gated entropy are defined in Methods and
    evaluated together in Results, against simple comparators and the
    partition index, and the claims are narrowed accordingly: no boundary
    parameter we tested is robust, and the gated entropy is presented as a
    monotone entropy that ranks compounds almost as the share of targets with
    detected binding does;

  - every robustness check the reviewer requested has been added; and

  - recovery from small panels is evaluated for all scores, with the share of
    compounds that can still be scored.

Re-running every analysis during this revision also found coding errors that
change some reported numbers. These are listed at the end of the response
letter, which gives a point-by-point account of all changes.

The manuscript has not been submitted or published elsewhere. A preprint is
available on ChemRxiv (https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15001618/v1). All data
and code are publicly available at
https://github.com/polinavino/kinase-selectivity-definitions, and no
