Dear Professor Baumann,

I am pleased to submit the second revision of manuscript 7756455,
"Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization and Motivated Properties for a Well-Formed Measure", as a
Research Article (Full Paper).

I am grateful to the reviewer for a second constructive assessment. In
response, the manuscript has been revised as follows:

  - the analyses now use one reference target per human protein kinase,
    removing mutant and domain constructs of the same gene from Davis,
    duplicate complexes from Anastassiadis, and targets that Klaeger et al.
    do not annotate as protein kinases from Klaeger;

  - Results now opens with the analysis choices that change how the
    definitions relate, including the published entropy under two treatments
    of pairs without detected binding;

  - the motivated properties and the candidate measure are defined formally
    in Methods and evaluated in Results, against three simple comparators, and
    the claims are narrowed accordingly: no measure we assess is robust to the
    placement of the activity boundary, and the candidate is presented as a
    monotone entropy that ranks compounds almost as a count of detected
    targets does;

  - every robustness check the reviewer requested has been added, including
    correlation matrices under the published forms of the entropy and Gini,
    the Klaeger filter sensitivity, the Metz coverage distribution, the
    convention for undefined scores, and a comparison of dataset and
    definition effects on compounds shared by Davis and Klaeger;

  - recovery from small panels is evaluated for all measures, including the
    published entropy, with the share of compounds that can still be scored;
    and

  - a Supporting Information file now holds the results previously available
    only in the code repository, with a table giving the script behind every
    reported result.

Re-running every analysis during this revision also found coding errors that
change some reported numbers. These are listed at the end of the response
letter, which gives a point-by-point account of all changes.

The manuscript has not been submitted or published elsewhere. A preprint is
available on ChemRxiv (https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15001618/v1). All data
and code are publicly available at
https://github.com/polinavino/kinase-selectivity-definitions, and no
confidential data were used. I declare no conflict of interest, and the work
