Dear Professor Baumann,

I am pleased to submit a revised version of manuscript 7756455, now titled
"Definitional Instability in Kinase Inhibitor Selectivity: An Empirical
Characterization Across Four Datasets and Required Properties for a Well-Formed
Measure" (previously "Towards a Formal Definition of Kinase Inhibitor
Selectivity: Empirical Characterization of Definitional Instability and Proposed
Desiderata"), as a Research Article (Full Paper).

I am grateful to the reviewer for a constructive assessment. The manuscript has
been substantially revised in response, most notably by:

  - simplifying the language and removing the speculative passages identified,
    including deletion of the "Relation to Formal Methods" section and replacing
    the term "desiderata" with "required properties" throughout;

  - extending the analysis from two datasets to four (adding the Anastassiadis
    and Metz datasets, spanning three assay technologies), and explicitly
    motivating the exclusion of the Gao and Patricelli datasets;

  - deriving and explaining each selectivity definition from its original
    publication, with a new "Faithfulness to the original definitions"
    subsection and a supporting sensitivity check; and

  - introducing a concrete candidate measure that satisfies all four required
    properties, strengthening the contribution beyond the previous version.

A detailed point-by-point response to the reviewer's comments is provided in the
accompanying author response letter.

The manuscript has not been submitted or published elsewhere. A preprint is
available on ChemRxiv (https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15001618/v1). All data
and code are publicly available at
https://github.com/polinavino/kinase-selectivity-definitions, and no
confidential data were used. I declare no conflict of interest, and the work
received no specific external funding. The use of a large language model
assistant in preparing the manuscript is disclosed in the Acknowledgement.
