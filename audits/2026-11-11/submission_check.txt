Requirements checked, condensed

Full Papers: no fixed length limit; abstract 600-1000 characters. TOC: title,
authors, corresponding-author asterisk, at most 80 words or three sentences,
and graphic at most 5 cm wide. Keywords: at most five, alphabetical, with two
from the catalogue. Supply contact metadata, editable equations/tables, figure
legends together after references, funding/conflict declarations and a data
statement. For plots Wiley prefers vector PDF or 600-dpi artwork, while review
acceptability depends on legibility. Cite the sources above for these rules.
